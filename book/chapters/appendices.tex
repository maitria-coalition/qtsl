\chapter{Proofs from Along the Way}
\label{ch:appendices}

\stubbanner

\stubplan Mathematical results produced during the toolkit's
development that QTSL soundness does not need: each appendix states
the result, its proof, and --- so the exile is honest --- exactly why
the soundness chain doesn't depend on it (superseded by a narrower
lemma, supporting a road not taken, or simply interesting). Admission
criterion: a result enters only with its non-dependence stated.

\stubmaterial Candidates identified at scaffold time, selection open: a
two-functoriality result for a double-Grothendieck construction; an
effective sparse Handelman positivity note; and a body of eigenmode
enclosure work (certified eigenvalue enclosures for the spectral
proposers).

\stubstatus Candidate list only; the selection pass belongs after the
Manual's coverage audit, which is what determines the
needed/not-needed boundary these appendices sit outside.
