/** The `producerT` spelling of unbounded reach: `F phi` compiled as
 * `F_{<=horizon} phi` at a producer-chosen horizon — a sound

/// The `producerT` spelling of unbounded reach: `F phi` compiled as
/// `F_{<=horizon} phi` at a producer-chosen horizon — a sound
