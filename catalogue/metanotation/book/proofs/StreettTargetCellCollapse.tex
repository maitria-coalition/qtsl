\begin{brecipe}[the filled-in application --- one row to one row]
\label{rec:tcc-filled}
Inside a \rulename{QStreettBundle} kit, fix a pair $i$ and a guard
transition with reset map $g$ and guard slab $\mathrm{slab}$, and let $T$
be a region on which the convention $W_i \equiv 0$ holds (the target
cells). Under the recomputed containment $g(\mathrm{slab}) \subseteq T$,
the rewrite is
\[
\bigl\{\vdash W_i \circ g \le W_i \ \text{on } \mathrm{slab}\bigr\}
\quad\rightsquigarrow\quad
\bigl\{\vdash W_i \ge 0 \ \text{on } \mathrm{slab}\bigr\}
\]
--- the ranking-guard row on the left is discharged by citing the
nonnegativity row on the right, which the bundle kit demands anyway. No
new row is emitted; the collapse is a citation.
\end{brecipe}

\begin{bprop}[the exactness identity --- one line]\label{prop:tcc-exact}
For every $y \in \mathrm{slab}$: if $g(y) \in T$ and $W_i \equiv 0$ on
$T$ and $0 \le W_i(y)$, then $W_i(g(y)) = 0 \le W_i(y)$.
\end{bprop}
\begin{proof}
Substitution --- the displayed chain is the proof. The guard row's left
side is identically zero on the slab, so the guard row and the nonneg
row assert the same inequality there.
\end{proof}

\begin{bprop}[soundness corollary]\label{prop:tcc-sound}
Any bundle kit admitting the nonneg row on the slab admits the
ranking-guard row on the slab; hence every conclusion drawn through
\rulename{QStreettBundle} from the collapsed kit is drawn from the full
kit, with the bundle's own soundness theorem as warrant.
\end{bprop}
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:tcc-exact} produces the guard row pointwise from
the nonneg row under the side-condition; the bundle theorem consumes rows
pointwise. There is no other content. (Machine-checked as
\texttt{ranking\_guard\_collapse}; not hash-bound in this edition.)
\end{proof}

\begin{bremark}[why the side-condition is not decoration]
Drop the containment and the collapse is unsound: a reset landing at a
state with $W_i(g(y)) > W_i(y)$ (outside the zero region) breaks the
ranking argument exactly where the bundle's telescoping lemma consumes
the guard row --- the certificate could be pumped back up at each guard
crossing, and the summability of un-responded requests
(\rulename{QStreettBundle} section) fails. The containment is
checker-recomputed for precisely this reason; its weight is the tier
flag recorded in the commentary.
\end{bremark}
